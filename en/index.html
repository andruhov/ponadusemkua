About

Who we are

“Mykolaiv First” (Миколаїв понад усе) is a local group of volunteers from Mykolaiv, Ukraine. We help people who remain in the Mykolaiv region to survive the hardships of war. We also provide much needed equipment to the defenders of the city.

Mykolaiv sits near the southern front. The city was shelled for a long time with artillery and missiles, including banned cluster munitions. Ukrainian forces pushed the occupiers back beyond the Dnipro — but civilians and soldiers still need support: vests, helmets, medical kits, and the logistics that keep aid moving.

Your donations can — and will — save lives. Literally.
